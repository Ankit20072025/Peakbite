Why we made Peakbite

It started in a hostel room. Gym in the evening, exams the next morning, and every protein bar on the shelf was either too sweet or too chalky to finish.

We wanted something simple: real protein, real ingredients, and flavours from the sweets and snacks we grew up with. Peakbite is that idea, built by students and tested on our friends first.

Questions

Is Peakbite a real company?

Peakbite is a student brand built for a digital marketing course. The website is live and the idea is real, but the bars are still a concept, so please treat orders as a demo.

Is it vegetarian?

Yes, every flavour is planned to be 100% vegetarian.

Will it make me bulk up?

No. A bar is a snack with 20g of protein, not magic. It works best next to real meals and regular training.

How long will a box last?

We are aiming for a 9-month shelf life from the date of making.

How would delivery work?

We are planning 3 to 5 working days across India. Message us and we will confirm for your city.
